Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

1. General Information

This Privacy Policy explains how I handle data on my personal website. As a personal resume site, I aim to keep data collection to an absolute minimum.

2. Cookies and Tracking

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with my site. This helps me improve the content and user experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as the number of visitors to the site, which pages they visit, and where they came from.

These cookies are only activated if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.

3. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. Since I do not collect personal identifiers directly (like names or emails) unless you contact me through external platforms (LinkedIn, Skype), your primary way to manage privacy here is by accepting or declining cookies.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out to me via LinkedIn.
